# Unknown-form recovery qualification — frozen protocol

This is a successor experiment, not a retroactive change to the successful coefficient-recovery qualification at 37c271a746842caf907d4555c7de8b3ae5615efe. The mathematical target is the existing Alpöge–Buckmaster lower arctangent estimate, transferred to OpenAI's genuine polar-chart interface. The benchmark designer knows the target. The synthesis process must not read its statement, proof body, or a prelisted target expression; the verifier may use the target only to return exact separating valuations. The source theorem is consulted directly only in the final comparison. This is bounded form recovery, not unrestricted theorem discovery.

## Frozen grammar and budget

The generic expression grammar is one linear monomial minus one correction monomial, `a*x - c*x^n`, generated from `X`, rational scalar multiplication, subtraction, and integer powers. The slope is chosen from {0, 1/4, 1/2, 3/4, 1}; the exponent from {2, 3, 4, 5}; the correction coefficient is an unknown nonnegative rational, not enumerated. The source exponent and coefficient are not supplied as a distinguished candidate. Enumerate all 20 syntax choices in their canonical order, with exponent increasing inside each slope. Deduplicate identical resulting expressions. No extra constructors, coefficients, or candidates may be introduced after seeing the result.

Available analytic facts: arctan(0)=0, derivative 1/(1+x^2), exact rational polynomial arithmetic, differentiation, positivity of 1+x^2, and monotonicity from nonnegative derivative. For each expression solve the exact coefficient inequalities for the least nonnegative c that makes `P=(1+x^2)*(1-L')` coefficientwise nonnegative. Reject a form if these sufficient inequalities are inconsistent. This certificate language is deliberately incomplete: rejection does not prove that no valid estimate of that form exists. A generated proof must use the solved expression and certificate; it may not import the withheld lower estimate. A failed proof does not permit changing the grammar or oracle.

The frozen `experiment.develop` is the only selection controller. Its initial capability is the known upper estimate. The residual mode receives the verifier's separating pair; the blind mode receives only pass/fail. Both use the same certified candidate set, candidate order, and a budget of two added features. The new operator generates and certifies candidates; it does not modify `develop`. Exhaust every ordering of the deduplicated certified candidates. Report the number of candidate forms examined, rejected, and retained, all ordering counts, selected expression, certificate, verifier calls, and witness trace. A candidate certificate is not accepted merely because finite tests pass.

## Frozen data and verification

The selector's training valuations are `(x,a)=(1,2/3),(1,7/12),(1,2)`. The protected consequence is the existing source-backed two-sided angle predicate; the observation is x. The upper estimate is initially installed. Held-out valuations are the Cartesian product of x in {0,1/2,1,2} and a in {0,1/4,1/2,7/12,2/3,3/4,1,3/2,2,3}. These are arbitrary rational valuations, not approximate arctangent samples. The oracle may evaluate the protected consequence but the synthesis module receives only returned witnesses, not the target expression.

The successful result must include: an exact finite obstruction; a generated expression and derivative certificate; a fresh Lean proof of the recovered universal estimate; standard-axiom checks; held-out finite adequacy and removal ablation; transfer to both `baseChart` and `localChart` in the pinned OpenAI source; and a post-freeze source comparison in pinned Alpöge–Buckmaster Lean.
