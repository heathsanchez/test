# Procedure composition v1 — frozen protocol

Parent: c4356c25a9e94f4b1e79d0c2bf53705694ec51f6. The previous qualification is immutable. This successor tests construction of a reusable certificate program, not selection among the four supplied finished builders. The original developmental selector is unchanged. Its adapter may receive only exact verifier residuals, and every semantic candidate test must be charged to the same budget in both arms.

The supplied language contains rational arithmetic, polynomial normalization and exact coefficient matching, a nonnegative constant, a nonnegative monomial on a ray, the square of an affine expression, an affine expression certified by its two interval endpoints, and sound sum/product composition. It contains no named half-line-square or interval-affine repair builder. A bounded structural search must assemble a certificate-program tree and compile it into a callable matcher. The program, rather than merely its first certificate, is the retained capability. The generic soundness of the tree interpreter is checked independently in Lean. Any symbolic factorization or parameter solving is an explicitly supplied algebraic operation, not an invented primitive.

The original obligations are P=x(x-1)^2/2 on the nonnegative ray and Q=1-x^2 on [0,1]. The first is used for synthesis and the second is withheld until after the first program is installed. A second, distinct program must be constructed if the first cannot certify Q. Reuse is tested on previously unlisted rational polynomials in the respective families. The original source-derived arctangent estimate is not imported into generated proofs.

Freeze the primitive grammar, candidate enumeration, maximum tree size, arithmetic bounds, and total semantic-check budget before execution. Both arms receive the same candidates, initial checker, domains and budgets. The residual arm may use exact failed coefficients and domain constraints to select a next program; the no-residual arm follows the frozen enumeration. Every candidate solve, certificate replay and verifier call is recorded. No candidate may be tested for free before selection. Structural pruning is permitted only when justified by a necessary condition recorded in the residual. Report CPU time separately: equal certificate-check budgets do not imply equal computational cost.

Acceptance requires an initial failure, an actual generated program AST, independently checked generic soundness, a successful original obligation, held-out reuse, and removal ablation. Report both arms' results at the same total-check cutoff. Failure is a valid result. A passing result establishes bounded construction of reusable proof programs from supplied primitives, not invention of the primitives, unrestricted self-development, or repair of an essential Navier–Stokes blow-up lemma. Any mathematical or search-grammar change after observing results requires a new experiment version.
