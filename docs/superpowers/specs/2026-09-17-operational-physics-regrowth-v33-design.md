# Operational Physics Regrowth V33 — Design

## Objective
Test whether three structures deleted from the scaffold regrow as minimum exact repairs under separately frozen operational obligations: positive/Gram structure from lawful composition, multiplicative parallel composition from mixture/unit/null laws, and noncommutative sequential operator structure from future consequences.

## Claim boundary
V33 is a finite exact reconstruction experiment. It does not derive physics from nothing, establish nature's ontology, derive Born's rule, complex Hilbert space, a C*-algebra, spacetime, or the Standard Model. Every positive claim is relative to the declared finite candidate grammar and frozen operational obligations.

## Arena P — positivity from composition
Candidates are normalized symmetric rational event-pairing matrices. Local admissibility requires nonnegative weights for every subset event. The rule grammar is ordered by complexity: local-only; local plus all proper principal minors; local plus all principal minors. Composition attacks search exact signed-integer directions v with v^T D v < 0 and nonzero sum, construct the normalized rank-one PSD companion vv^T/(sum v)^2, and use the diagonal joint event to replay the negative weight. The retained rule is the cheapest grammar rule that accepts protected positive controls and excludes every generated composition residual.

## Arena T — parallel composition
The candidate grammar is biaffine scalar combiners f(a,b)=c0+c1 a+c2 b+c3 ab with coefficients in {-1,0,1}. Frozen obligations are mixture affinity, null-event laws f(0,b)=f(a,0)=0, and unit-event laws f(1,b)=b, f(a,1)=a on exact rational probes. The selected minimum combiner must be unique, associative on probes, and induce the Kronecker product on basis event-pairing matrices.

## Arena S — sequential structure
Operational states are exact rational vectors. Candidate representation families are scalar, two-dimensional diagonal, and full two-dimensional linear operators. A frozen hidden pair of linear operations has order-sensitive future signatures. The learner selects the cheapest family that exactly replays all protected sequence observations. The expected positive result is only a noncommutative linear operator algebra with composition by function composition; quantum/Jordan/C*-specific structure is not predeclared and is not claimed.

## Cross-arena result
The runner reports which structures were independently forced, their residual witnesses, ablations, and the strongest unforced boundary. No arena may use another arena's target label as a premise.

## Verification
Standard-library Python only; Fractions for exact arithmetic; unit tests include positive controls, composition counterexample replay, grammar ablations, relabel/order controls, and explicit negative claim tests.
