# Blind Heterogeneous World Stream V1 — frozen protocol

## Question

Can the unchanged Emergent Persistence Hierarchy V1 controller form a useful
hierarchy when its encounters arrive through heterogeneous verifier-backed task
interfaces, with neither domain labels nor family labels exposed to it?

## Frozen intervention

The controller, promotion rule, cost law, canonical identity rule, and regime
shift logic are imported byte-for-byte from the frozen predecessor experiment.
Only an adapter layer is added. Four finite domains (equational rewriting,
program repair, abstract transformation, and planning) encode the same generic
trace language with disjoint carriers and vocabularies. Each adapter must pass
exhaustive encode/decode and outcome-profile checks before its trace can enter
the controller. Adapter verification is charged equally in every condition.

The 32 encounters, their opaque encodings, and the shift at encounter 13 are
fixed in source. Domain and family metadata are retained only by the external
evaluator and are never passed to the controller.

## Primary prospective gate

At the instant each structure is promoted, freeze its depth. On the unseen
suffix, measure (a) the number of future encounters in which it occurs and (b)
the number of distinct verifier domains reached. Mean future reuse radius and
mean ablation breadth must be nondecreasing with learned depth, with a strict
increase from the shallowest to deepest observed depth.

## Controls

Compare cold, adaptive, deep ablation, shallow ablation, sham state, and answer
memory. A structure-destroying scramble retains the domain schedule, trace
shapes, surface-volume distribution, and per-episode root counts, but replaces
recurring complete authority profiles with encounter-specific profiles. It must
not produce the same depth/reuse hierarchy or the adaptive saving.

## Claim boundary

This is a finite, constructed, verifier-backed heterogeneous-stream
qualification. It does not establish hierarchy formation in natural data,
open-ended domains, unbounded recursion, or optimality of the controller.
