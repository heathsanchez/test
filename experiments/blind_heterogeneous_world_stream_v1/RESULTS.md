# Blind Heterogeneous World Stream V1 — result

## Verdict

`NEGATIVE_OR_PARTIAL` under the finite constructed verifier-backed prospective
qualification.

The unchanged controller formed depths 0, 1, and 2 and reduced fully charged
cost from 2775 to 973 (2.852x). All four opaque finite adapters passed complete
verification. Deep ablation was broader than the selected shallow ablation, and
the structure-destroying scramble eliminated both hierarchy and savings.

The precommitted primary gate failed. Mean prospective reuse was 18.4, 25.0,
and 19.0 future episodes at depths 0, 1, and 2 respectively; mean ablation
breadth was 16.4, 23.0, and 19.0. Thus dependency depth did not monotonically
predict prospective reuse radius.

## Interpretation

This run verifies that the frozen amortizing controller remains operational and
economically useful behind heterogeneous verifier interfaces. It does **not**
show that emergent dependency depth tracks scale of generalization. In fact it
exposes a structural reason for caution: a shallow dependency can be reused by
every deeper construction and therefore have broader reach than its dependents.
Dependency depth, persistence, and generalization radius are distinct empirical
coordinates; they cannot be silently identified.

No natural-world ecological-validity claim is supported.
