# Emergent Persistence Geometry V1 — frozen protocol

## Hypothesis

Dependency depth, warranted persistence, prospective reuse scope, causal
ablation magnitude, and accumulated amortized value are separable coordinates
of retained structure. The frozen hierarchy controller must not be changed.

## Design

Four matched, finite prospective interventions use opaque verifier profiles:

1. **Ancestry:** replace a depth-0 structure by a depth-2 composite while
   holding its encounter schedule and domain scope fixed.
2. **Scope:** hold structure, timing, and call volume fixed while changing only
   which opaque verifier domains instantiate it.
3. **Persistence:** hold structure and encounter schedule fixed while moving the
   complete authority-certified invalidation boundary.
4. **Causal breadth:** hold structure, active episodes, domains, and warrant
   fixed while changing the number of verified computations depending on it.

Every condition imports the predecessor controller byte-for-byte. Stable
identity is the digest of a complete authority profile. Domain labels remain in
the external evaluator only. Adapter and authority checks are fully charged.

## Primary gates

Each intervention must selectively move its intended coordinate: ancestry moves
`d`; scope moves `R`; invalidation moves `tau`; dependent multiplicity moves
`B`. At least two crossed orderings must occur, proving that no one coordinate
is a monotone proxy for all the others. Adaptive execution must retain a fully
charged advantage over cold across the matched suite.

This qualification is finite and constructed. It cannot establish natural-world
geometry, open-ended discovery, or optimality of the coordinate definitions.
