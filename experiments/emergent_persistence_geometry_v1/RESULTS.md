## Interpretation

Within this constructed domain, dependency depth, warranted persistence,
prospective reuse scope, and causal magnitude are not a scalar hierarchy. They
are independently addressable coordinates of a proof-carrying developmental
graph. Accumulated amortized value was recorded as a fifth coordinate but its
optimal predictive role was not tested.

This result does not establish statistical independence, natural-world
emergence, or an optimal retention-value estimator.
