# V28 Retained-Capability Causal Compounding — Frozen Protocol

## Question

Does promotion of the V16–V25 ALL_ACTIVE incidence law into retained execution cause a measurable reduction in future developmental work, rather than merely restating a theorem?

V28 freezes a new finite world that was not used to select the law: one seven-ary relation on a two-element carrier, with exactly one true tuple. The complete world therefore contains all 2^7 = 128 possible tuples.

## Cold developmental condition

The only varying representation choice is which of the seven active argument-position incidence channels are connected to the tuple-occurrence node. Identity anchors and role colors are fixed.

Exhaust all 2^7 = 128 channel subsets. For every candidate, compare source isomorphism with represented equivalence on all 128^2 ordered object pairs.

A cold pass requires:
- the entire 128-candidate family is exhausted;
- exactly one candidate has zero semantic disagreement;
- that candidate is ALL_ACTIVE, containing all seven channels;
- removing any one channel from ALL_ACTIVE restores disagreement.

No candidate ordering is used to claim the gain; cold cost is the complete-family qualification cost.

## Warm retained condition

Instantiate the already-retained ALL_ACTIVE law directly.

Operational representation-search candidates: 0.
Qualification evaluates the retained representation once on the same complete world.

A warm pass requires zero disagreement and exact equality with the unique cold survivor.

## Causal ablation

Run the frozen controller in three conditions:

1. retained capability present -> EXECUTE_REUSE, no developmental search;
2. retained capability removed, development enabled -> DEVELOP, exhaustive cold reconstruction, then PROMOTE;
3. retained capability removed, development disabled -> UNKNOWN.

This distinguishes causal reuse from mere availability.

## Held-out continuation

After the arity-7 qualification is sealed, apply the retained law without search to an arity-9 challenge on a three-element carrier. Two unary marker relations fix source identities; the nine-ary relation contains previously unseen tuples.

Enumerate all six carrier permutations independently:
- a positive target must admit a source isomorphism;
- a negative target formed by perturbing one nine-ary tuple must admit none.

The retained ALL_ACTIVE representation must agree with the source verdicts with zero DEVELOPMENT transitions and zero representation search.

## Pass

A pass requires:
- unique ALL_ACTIVE adequacy on the complete arity-7 world;
- every single-channel ablation fails;
- warm and cold semantics agree;
- cold/warm qualification comparison ratio is exactly 128x;
- retained-capability ablation restores developmental search;
- disabling both retention and development returns UNKNOWN;
- held-out arity-9 positive and negative source/representation verdicts agree.

## Claim boundary

This establishes bounded causal compounding of a previously developed and formally justified relational capability. It does not establish autonomous subject selection, unrestricted invention of developmental meta-operations, or unbounded action-language genesis.
