# Joint continuation and invariant construction v1

## Frozen claim

Test whether a residual can construct an unnamed separating continuation from a
fixed low-level grammar, induce a quotient solely from complete consequence
profiles, and causally enable prospective transfer to non-isomorphic carriers.

The continuation grammar contains only three field tests and `not`, `and`, and
`or`. It contains no `xor`, parity, separator, quotient, class map, or named
candidate continuation. Enumeration is level-complete through depth four and
deduplicated only by complete extensional behavior.

All construction evaluations and bridge-verifier calls count. Four target
carriers are frozen before execution. The separator and quotient are hidden as
derived values: the residual provides required pairwise consequences, the
grammar constructs a matching expression, and equivalence classes are computed
from resulting profiles.

Controls are cold, joint warm, separator ablation, certificate ablation, matched
sham, and answer memory. The positive gate requires correctness, fully charged
warm improvement, and exact restoration by both separator and certificate
ablation.

This protocol cannot establish open-ended discovery or grammar genesis. Those
require a separate complete negative over the present grammar.
