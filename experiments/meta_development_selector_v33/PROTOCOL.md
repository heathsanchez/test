# Meta-Development Selector V33 — Frozen Protocol

## Residual inherited from V32
V32 passed every behavioral gate but violated its own executable-vocabulary purity condition because the active test selector was named `select_intervention`. V33 repairs that protocol defect before new hidden worlds exist.

V33 also strengthens developmental compilation. V32 cached exact Boolean operator identities inside recurring developmental skeletons. V33 compiles only structural shape, offset pattern, and variable recurrence. Every binary operator position is replaced by an anonymous wildcard and is re-exhausted during warm replay.

Thus a recurring structural move may transfer across a different unseen finite law instead of memorizing the old law.

## Frozen substrate
A record is a finite binary trace over anonymous channels and offsets. The executable kernel receives no evaluator-side semantic category.

The only constructors are K(b), A(offset,channel), and exhaustive two-input Boolean B(op,left,right), recursively composed to the bounded three-access, depth-two frontier.

## Selection law
A candidate is verifier-clean exactly when consequence is constant inside its induced signature classes. Exact minima are ordered lexicographically by:

1. consequence-relative block count;
2. raw access count;
3. constructor count;
4. depth;
5. total offset.

No fitted coefficients are used. All exact minima are retained.

## Structural developmental compilation
Solved expressions are channel-renaming normalized. During compilation, every B operator identity is erased to `*`. Only schemas recurring across independently supplied worlds are retained.

Warm replay instantiates channel variables and exhausts every operator wildcard. With acquisition disabled it may not run cold semantic closure.

## Active discrimination
If several exact minima remain, the kernel may inspect unlabeled candidate records only. It selects the record maximizing distinct predictions across the surviving frontier. Hidden consequence is supplied only after the record is selected.

## Required prospective tests
After freeze, unseen challenge worlds must test:

- no change;
- one-access current and delayed structures;
- pair structures governed by Boolean laws different from the training laws;
- mixed-offset pair structure;
- three-access nested composition;
- context-, action-, and joint-style evaluator interpretations that are not visible to the kernel;
- active discrimination of a noncanonical minimum frontier;
- consequence-preserving contraction;
- incomplete authority;
- cold-versus-warm acquisition economics;
- developmental-store ablation.

A strong transfer result requires warm structural replay to recover the same minimum class as cold exhaustive search on at least one held-out pair law not present in the training operator set, with zero acquisition search and fewer tested candidates.

## Executable-vocabulary gate
The V33 kernel source must not contain evaluator repair-class tokens: memory, relation, context, intervention, joint, quantum, symmetry, or grain.

## Claim boundary
A pass is a bounded finite result about structural meta-compilation, residual-driven anonymous representation search, active disambiguation, and verified contraction. It does not establish unrestricted ontology invention, universal causal discovery, or reconstruction of a physical theory.
