# Persistence Correspondence Genesis V39 — Frozen Protocol

## Question

Can cross-time recurrence correspondence be removed as a supplied primitive?

V39 receives two finite anonymous local frames, BEFORE and AFTER.

Within each frame, occurrences are locally addressable only for the duration of the encounter. The scientific kernel is given no map saying which BEFORE occurrence is "the same thing" as which AFTER occurrence.

The kernel must decide whether any cross-time correspondence is required at all, and if so which minimum partial matching between the two frames is forced by verified consequence.

The hidden challenge pack is committed only after this scientific core is frozen.

## Zero-correspondence baseline

With zero cross-time links, the representation retains only:

- the unordered multiset of BEFORE values;
- the unordered multiset of AFTER values.

No persistence claim is made.

If this independent-bag quotient exactly matches authoritative consequence, the kernel must stop at zero links.

## Candidate persistence language

A candidate recurrence hypothesis is a partial injective matching

    M ⊆ BEFORE × AFTER.

Each BEFORE occurrence and each AFTER occurrence may appear in at most one link.

For a transition row, a candidate matching induces:

- the unordered multiset of linked value-pairs;
- the unordered multiset of unmatched BEFORE values;
- the unordered multiset of unmatched AFTER values.

Thus frame labels are not exported into the consequence quotient.

Search is exhaustive in increasing number of cross-time links.

## Generic interventions

Some worlds additionally contain one local BEFORE intervention marker and one local AFTER response marker.

The kernel is not told that these two occurrences are the same persistent entity.

For an interventional row, a candidate correspondence contributes exactly one extra generic bit:

    does the candidate matching contain
    (intervention_target, response_occurrence)?

This is the only use of intervention identity.

It does not name an object, role, or semantic causal type. It tests whether the proposed recurrence relation carries the observed intervention-response pairing.

Passive rows carry no such bit.

## Scientific purpose of the intervention control

For three occurrences, a two-link passive matching contains a real observational symmetry.

Certain distinct two-link matchings induce exactly the same passive consequence quotient.

V39 preserves that non-identifiability as a required result.

The corresponding active world adds intervention/response evidence. If that evidence is sufficient, the same frozen kernel should collapse the passive minimum frontier to the unique hidden cross-time matching.

Thus the experiment tests both:

1. refusal to manufacture identity when passive consequence cannot identify it;
2. construction of identity when intervention breaks the symmetry.

## Exact authority

Primary worlds use three BEFORE and three AFTER binary occurrences.

Passive worlds contain the complete 2^6 transition table.

Active worlds contain, for every binary transition:

- one passive row; and
- all 3 × 3 intervention-target/response-occurrence combinations.

Incomplete authority remains UNKNOWN_AUTHORITY.

## Search order

The structural cost is exactly:

    number of cross-time links.

All matchings at one link count are exhausted before another persistence link is authorized.

No scalar coefficients are tuned.

## Post-freeze challenge family

After freeze, hidden worlds will include:

- no cross-time persistence required;
- one required recurrence link;
- a passive two-link world with a certified two-member minimum frontier;
- the same two-link world with intervention/response evidence that resolves the frontier to one map;
- a complete three-link recurrence world;
- independently relabelled BEFORE and AFTER local frames;
- consequence-label relabellings.

The scientific kernel is never told the hidden maps.

## Frozen gates

P1. Frozen scientific core remains byte-identical.
P2. Zero-persistence world stops at zero links.
P3. One-link world exhausts the independent-bag baseline before earning exactly one correspondence.
P4. Passive two-link world has exactly two minimum matching witnesses and must remain non-identifiable.
P5. The two passive minimum witnesses belong to one abstract matching-size class but are distinct labelled correspondences.
P6. Adding generic intervention/response evidence to the same hidden two-link world collapses the frontier to the unique hidden correspondence.
P7. The active two-link solution retains exactly two links; intervention does not justify a spurious third identity.
P8. Full-persistence world requires exactly three cross-time links.
P9. Every cheaper matching count is exhausted before the three-link world is accepted.
P10. Independent BEFORE/AFTER frame permutations transform the recovered labelled correspondence equivariantly.
P11. Consequence-label relabelling preserves minimum link count and frontier structure.
P12. Zero-link ablation preserves the no-persistence world but leaves every persistence-requiring world CERTIFIED_CORRESPONDENCE_INADEQUACY.
P13. Removing intervention evidence from the active two-link challenge restores the passive two-member frontier.
P14. At least one rejected cheaper matching carries an explicit merge-obstruction or excess-distinction witness.
P15. Incomplete authority remains UNKNOWN_AUTHORITY and verifier ablation authorizes no correspondence search.
P16. Object/site/graph/relation names and hidden challenge maps are absent from the frozen executable kernel.

## Claim boundary

A pass would NOT establish persistence from completely structureless observations.

V39 still supplies two local occurrence frames and stable local coordinate identity within each frame.

What is removed is the cross-time identity relation itself.

The experiment can establish only the bounded claim:

> given anonymous local BEFORE/AFTER occurrence frames, verified transition consequence can decide whether cross-time identity is unnecessary, and when passive evidence is insufficient can preserve non-identifiability until generic intervention-response evidence forces a minimum correspondence.

If V39 passes, the remaining handhold is local occurrence individuation inside each frame. The next frontier would be to remove even those stable within-frame occurrence coordinates and derive local segmentation plus cross-time recurrence jointly.
