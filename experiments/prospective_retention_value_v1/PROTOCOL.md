# Prospective Retention Value V1 — frozen protocol

Test whether a geometry-aware value estimate, using only verified information
available at decision time, reduces fully charged future reconstruction under a
three-structure persistent-state budget. Compare oracle, accumulated
amortization, depth, persistence, observed scope, deterministic sham/random, and
unlimited retention. The 48-episode stream crosses genealogy, lifetime, scope,
causal saving, genesis cost, delayed value, and revocation. Hidden schedules and
future invalidations are inaccessible to every non-oracle policy.

The primary gate is that prospective geometry-aware retention beats every
one-coordinate policy. At episode 24, removing its highest-valued retained
structure must cause at least as much verified suffix reconstruction as removing
the nodes selected by the depth, persistence, scope, or amortization heuristics.
All verifier, reconstruction, maintenance, admission, and revocation charges are
included. This is a finite constructed qualification, not a learned natural-world
forecast or proof of estimator optimality.
