# Quantum Joint Grain Genesis V31 — Frozen Protocol

## Dependency

V30 established a classical control:
measurement disturbance, operation-relative grains, and order dependence can all arise without quantum mechanics.

V31 therefore tests a stronger boundary that the V30 control cannot certify:

    local descriptions are insufficient,
    but a joint quantum representation exactly predicts the observed statistics.

The scientific target is Bell-type nonlocal correlation plus constructive quantum replay.

## Question

Can verified consequence force a joint representation when no Bell-local model can reproduce the observations, while a finite generic two-qubit quantum hypothesis language can?

The target is:

    local marginals
      -> unresolved / coarse local description
      -> joint measurement statistics
      -> Bell-local obstruction
      -> exact finite quantum hypothesis search
      -> minimum non-product joint state
      -> independent Born-rule replay.

## Primitive observation interface

The learner receives an anonymous bipartite two-setting/two-outcome experiment:

    x in {0,1}    local setting on side A
    y in {0,1}    local setting on side B
    a in {-1,+1}  local outcome on A
    b in {-1,+1}  local outcome on B

and exact joint probabilities:

    P(a,b | x,y).

No words such as entanglement, Bell state, spin, quark, particle, collapse, or nonlocality occur in the hidden challenge data.

The kernel is given arbitrary 2x2 real local observables A_x and B_y as exact matrices over Q(sqrt(2)).

## Exact arithmetic

All probabilities and expectation values are represented in the exact quadratic field

    Q(sqrt(2)) = {r + s sqrt(2) : r,s in Q}.

No floating-point tolerance determines acceptance.

## Local / Bell-local obstruction

First compute:
- local marginals P(a|x), P(b|y);
- correlators E_xy = sum_ab a b P(a,b|x,y).

Then exhaust all eight CHSH sign forms:

    S = +/-E_00 +/-E_01 +/-E_10 +/-E_11

with sign product -1.

Any Bell-local hidden-variable model satisfies:

    |S| <= 2.

If the exact observed maximum exceeds 2, emit a certified Bell-local obstruction.

This excludes the whole Bell-local polytope for the 2x2x2 scenario, not merely product distributions.

## Generic finite quantum hypothesis language

The frozen quantum constructor does not contain a named Bell state.

It enumerates every nonzero real two-qubit coefficient vector

    v in {-1,0,+1}^4

modulo global sign.

Each candidate denotes the normalized pure state

    |psi_v> = v / ||v||.

The coefficient alphabet and two-qubit carrier are declared finite substrate bounds.

A candidate is classified only after construction:
- product / Schmidt rank 1 iff det([[v0,v1],[v2,v3]]) = 0;
- joint / Schmidt rank 2 otherwise.

No candidate is generated by asking for an entangled state.

## Born verifier

For every candidate state and every supplied local observable pair:
- compute <A_x tensor I>;
- compute <I tensor B_y>;
- compute <A_x tensor B_y>;
- derive exact two-outcome joint probabilities by

    P(a,b|x,y)
      = 1/4 [
          1
          + a <A_x tensor I>
          + b <I tensor B_y>
          + ab <A_x tensor B_y>
        ].

A candidate is admitted only if every exact probability equals the hidden observation table.

## Grain test

Local-only evidence is the set of one-party marginals.

Joint evidence is the full P(a,b|x,y).

The kernel groups finite quantum hypotheses by observational equivalence under:
1. local evidence only;
2. full joint evidence.

If several distinct joint hypotheses remain identical locally but are separated by joint statistics, the local grain is certified too coarse.

The selected joint representation is the smallest exact surviving hypothesis class under declared structural cost:

    Schmidt rank first,
    then support size.

If no rank-1 candidate survives but a rank-2 candidate does, the constructive language is forced beyond independent pure products.

## Controls

1. Product-state control:
   a hidden rank-1 state must not violate CHSH and must be fit by a rank-1 candidate.

2. Joint-data ablation:
   using only marginals must retain ambiguity among multiple hypotheses.

3. Correlation ablation:
   replacing P(a,b|x,y) by the product of its marginals must remove the Bell obstruction.

4. Relabelling:
   permutations/sign relabellings of settings/outcomes must preserve the Bell-local/nonlocal classification and minimum Schmidt rank.

5. Wrong-state replay:
   a different finite candidate with identical local marginals but different joint correlations must fail full replay.

6. Incomplete authority:
   missing probability cells return UNKNOWN_AUTHORITY.

## Required post-freeze gates

Q1. Exact Q(sqrt(2)) arithmetic is used throughout verification.
Q2. Hidden entangled challenge has normalized nonnegative probabilities for every setting pair.
Q3. Its local marginals alone are maximally uninformative (1/2,1/2) on both sides/settings.
Q4. At least two distinct frozen candidate states share those local marginals.
Q5. Exact CHSH evaluation gives a value strictly greater than 2.
Q6. Therefore Bell-local adequacy is certified false.
Q7. Exhaustive frozen quantum search contains no exact rank-1 replay of the joint statistics.
Q8. At least one exact rank-2 candidate replays every joint probability.
Q9. Minimum surviving Schmidt rank is exactly 2.
Q10. Full joint evidence strictly refines the local-only hypothesis grain.
Q11. A wrong rank-2 candidate with the same local marginals fails joint replay.
Q12. Product-state control has CHSH <= 2 and an exact rank-1 replay.
Q13. Correlation ablation using product marginals has CHSH <= 2.
Q14. Setting/outcome relabelling preserves nonlocal classification and minimum Schmidt rank.
Q15. Incomplete probability authority returns UNKNOWN_AUTHORITY.
Q16. Removing joint evidence restores local ambiguity rather than guessing a unique state.
Q17. No named Bell-state constructor exists in the frozen candidate generator.
Q18. The V30 classical properties alone are not used as a quantum certificate; Bell-local obstruction is required.

## Scientific interpretation

A pass establishes, within this bounded exact two-qubit experiment:

> local marginal descriptions are provably inadequate; the observed joint statistics violate the Bell-local bound; and an independently replayed rank-2 quantum state from a generic finite hypothesis language exactly accounts for them.

This is a genuine quantum-structure test in a way V30 was not.

It does NOT establish:
- faster-than-light signalling;
- that nature is universally quantum because of this simulation;
- autonomous invention of Hilbert space, Born's rule, tensor products, or the verifier;
- unrestricted quantum-state learning;
- a metaphysical claim that relations are more fundamental than states.
