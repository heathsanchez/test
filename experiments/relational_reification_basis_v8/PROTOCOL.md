# Relational Reification Basis V8 — Frozen Protocol

## Question

Can the V7 finite proto-semantic basis be reduced by making PRODUCT and QUOTIENT *derived* constructions of a more primitive finite relation calculus plus the already-established developmental act of verified reification?

V7 found the inclusion-minimal basis, inside its declared candidate universe:

    BIT + PRODUCT + QUOTIENT + RELATION + COMPOSE

V8 asks whether PRODUCT and QUOTIENT are genuinely primitive, or whether they are two different ways of reifying relational structure.

## Frozen semantic seed

The object-level seed is only:

- BIT: one binary distinction space.

The candidate proto-semantic primitives are now only:

1. RELATION
   - a finite typed relation R : A <-> B represented extensionally as a set of source-target incidences.
   - predicates are relations into BIT.
   - deterministic computations are functional relations.

2. COMPOSE
   - ordinary relational composition.

No PRODUCT carrier constructor and no QUOTIENT carrier constructor exist in the object language.

## Frozen developmental constitution

The developmental constitution already established before V8 remains outside the proto-semantic basis:

- external verification;
- exact finite bounded search;
- conservative UNKNOWN;
- behavior/evidence quotienting;
- verified REIFY/COMPILE.

V8 gives REIFY two generic, challenge-independent relational forms:

### EDGE-REIFY
Given a verified relation R : A <-> B, construct a carrier whose elements are exactly the verified incidences (a,b) in R.

If R is the verified total relation A <-> B, the resulting carrier is extensionally equivalent to A x B, but PRODUCT is not an object-language primitive.

### CLASS-REIFY
Given a verified equivalence relation E : A <-> A, construct one carrier element per verified equivalence class.

If E partitions A into k classes, the resulting carrier has k elements, but QUOTIENT is not an object-language primitive.

These are treated as generic compilation of verified relational patterns into new distinction spaces. They are not available without a verified source relation.

## Why this is a genuine reduction test

A pass would show that, in the declared finite regime:

- product-like spaces can arise by reifying a total relation;
- quotient-like spaces can arise by reifying equivalence classes;
- predicates, computation, modular composition, learned vocabulary, grammar/type formation, semantic carrier growth, and meta-rules can all be rebuilt above those operations.

This would reduce the V7 object-level primitive set from four operations to:

    RELATION + COMPOSE

with BIT as seed and verified reification in the developmental constitution.

## Required post-freeze ladder

The challenge pack is added only after the basis/kernel freeze.

### L0 proto-logic
Construct a nontrivial predicate on the reified edge carrier of the total BIT<->BIT relation.

### L1 finite mathematical structure
Construct a 3-element carrier by:
1. reifying total BIT<->BIT into a four-edge carrier;
2. constructing and verifying an equivalence relation on those four elements with exactly three classes;
3. class-reifying it.

### L2 computation
Construct and exhaustively execute a nontrivial functional relation.

### L3 modular computation
Compile two verified functional relation atoms and solve a new transfer by COMPOSE below the cost of direct extensional construction. Cold tight search must fail; deeper cold search may recover directly.

### L4 vocabulary
Repeated verified relation must reify into an anonymous relation atom and be reused with zero direct relation-search acquisition.

### L5 grammar/type formation
A recurring relational carrier-construction pattern must be reified as a formation macro and transferred to an unseen base carrier under a tighter construction budget than cold reconstruction.

### L6 semantic basis growth
A 3-element carrier must be impossible before class-reification and become available after verified equivalence-class reification; it must then support construction of a six-element carrier via edge-reification of the total relation C3<->BIT. Ablation of C3 must restore the obstruction.

### L7 meta-rule
A bounded developmental mapping must be represented by the same relation substrate; partial evidence must preserve a non-canonical frontier, future consequence must select, then verified compilation must enable zero-search reuse.

### UNKNOWN
Incomplete search must remain UNKNOWN.

## Exhaustive primitive-subset test

Run every subset of:

    {RELATION, COMPOSE}

against the entire ladder.

Report all passing subsets and inclusion-minimal passing subsets.

A primitive is causally necessary only if its ablation restores at least one failed gate under the same frozen challenge ladder.

## Claim boundary

A pass establishes only:

> within the declared finite ladder, PRODUCT and QUOTIENT do not need to be independent object-language primitives if verified relational reification is part of the developmental constitution.

It does not establish that relation is metaphysically primitive, that reification is irreducible, or that the same basis handles continuous/natural domains.

The next boundary is whether reification itself can be reduced to consequential difference plus recurrence/selection, and whether temporal recurrence can be derived without supplying frequency, cycle, or phase.
