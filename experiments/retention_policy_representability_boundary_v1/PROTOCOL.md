# Retention-Policy Representability Boundary V1

## Frozen question

Does the frozen 6,561-program grammar contain a policy behaviorally identical to the supplied geometry policy over every frozen reachable decision state?

## Exhaustive fork

- **Representable but unselected:** diagnose an authority/value-evidence residual. Grammar growth is forbidden.
- **Not representable:** emit a policy-language expressivity residual and identify minimal separating states before proposing a new primitive.

The experiment enumerates every policy, evaluates its complete decision signature over all 48 frozen states, and records every divergence between the previously selected policy and geometry. No new stream, feature, coefficient, or policy primitive is introduced.
