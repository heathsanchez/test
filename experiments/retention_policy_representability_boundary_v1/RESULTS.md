# Retention-Policy Representability Boundary V1 — Result

## Verdict

**REPRESENTABLE BUT UNSELECTED.** Exhaustive evaluation of all 6,561 programs over all 48 frozen reachable decision states found exactly one program behaviorally identical to the hand geometry policy: the hand geometry coefficient vector itself.

The selected policy was rational under the construction authority: it cost 20,000 on the four training streams versus 20,341 for geometry. On the twenty held-out streams the ordering reversed: 104,887 versus 102,558.

Therefore the previous gap is not a retention-policy-language limitation. It is a selection-objective / future-value-evidence residual. Grammar extension is not licensed.

There were 15 decision divergences across the complete frozen state set. The first held-out divergence occurred at seed 102, where the generated decision cost 6,973 and geometry cost 5,357.
