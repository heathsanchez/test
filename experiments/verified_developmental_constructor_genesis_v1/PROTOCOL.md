# Verified Developmental-Constructor Genesis V1 — Frozen Protocol

## Question

Can certified failure of every current developmental operator cause synthesis of a reusable operator that constructs new coordinates, when no quotient/add-coordinate developmental operator is supplied by name?

## Frozen objects

- Raw carrier: all 16 four-bit records. The object representation initially exposes only field 3.
- Current developmental language: every unary Boolean recoding of the exposed field (four extensional operators). This language is finite and exhaustively complete.
- Training residuals: pairs equal in the exposed field but separated respectively by raw fields 0 and 1.
- Prospective held-out residual: a pair equal in fields 0, 1, and 3 but separated by field 2. It is evaluated only after the developmental operator is frozen.
- Lower-level generative substrate: finite iteration, raw-field projection, verifier-provided `Separates` predicate, ordered first-match selection, product pairing, and composition. It contains no named quotient, coordinate-growth, history-state, or target-field operator.
- Meta-program synthesis obligation: construct a single program which, for each training residual, preserves the old representation and pairs it with a verifier-certified separating projection.
- Candidate programs are generated structurally from the substrate; the exact developmental operator is not listed as a candidate.

## Controls

- Ablation: remove the synthesized developmental operator.
- Sham: matched-size operator always adds training field 0.
- Answer memory: retain the two training repairs/selected field indices but not the synthesized selection procedure.
- Target-specific repair: forbidden; the held-out field is not embedded in the frozen operator.

## Pass

Pass requires an exhaustive negative over all four old developmental operators, typed developmental `UNKNOWN_EXPRESSIVITY`, construction and verification of a program from lower primitives, success on both training residuals, untouched success on the held-out residual, preservation of the old representation, and loss of held-out repair under ablation, sham, and answer-memory controls.

## Claim boundary

A pass earns **BOUNDED, EXHAUSTIVE, CAUSAL DEVELOPMENTAL-CONSTRUCTOR GENESIS** within the declared finite meta-program substrate. It does not establish invention of iteration, projection, pairing, or separation testing themselves.
