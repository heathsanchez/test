# Verified Generator Construction V1 — Frozen Protocol

## Question

Can a proof-carrying developmental controller, after an exhaustive negative over its active language, construct (rather than select by name) a new generator from residual constraints in a frozen generic generative substrate, admit it, preserve old consequence, solve a frozen reuse target, and fail again under ablation?

## Frozen objects

- Carrier: all 16 Boolean functions of two inputs, encoded as four-bit truth tables.
- Initial atoms: `0`, `1`, `x`, `y`; active constructor: pointwise `not`.
- Trigger target: `x and y` (mask 8).
- Prospective reuse target: `x and not y` (mask 4). It is checked only after admission.
- Generic meta-language: an unnamed binary Boolean truth table with four initially unbound output bits, synthesized from verifier-derived row constraints. There is no list of named candidate operators.
- Synthesis rule: enumerate input rows canonically; bind each previously unbound table entry to the externally supplied target output; reject inconsistent constraints; leave no unconstrained entry. The generated artifact is its four-bit table plus provenance, not a symbolic operator name.
- Authority: exhaustive equality on all four Boolean input rows.
- Cost: every old-language membership check, generator-row verification, preservation check, target/reuse check, and ablation check is charged as one verifier call.

## Precommitted gates

Pass requires all of the following: initial `UNKNOWN_SEARCH`; exact exhaustive old closure; certified negative for the trigger; typed `UNKNOWN_EXPRESSIVITY`; no named candidate menu; complete and consistent residual-derived construction; constructed generator outside the active language; exhaustive external verification; stale/incomplete/negative-missing/old-capability admission controls rejected; admission; trigger resolution; preservation of every old reachable function; success on the frozen reuse target; and restoration of both obstructions under ablation.

## Claim boundary

A pass earns **BOUNDED-EXHAUSTIVE-CAUSAL GENERATOR CONSTRUCTION** within the declared finite truth-table meta-language. It does not establish construction beyond that meta-language, unrestricted self-development, cross-domain transfer, or compounding.
