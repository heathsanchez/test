# Verified Prospective Repair Selection V1

All five minimal repair classes from the frozen parent qualification begin
equally available. The original residual supplies no preference.

Twenty-four prospective worlds are frozen by seed. Each applies an opaque
carrier permutation and returns a rich verifier vector: calls, later residuals,
additional growth, revocation, and correctness. The predeclared relation is
coordinatewise Pareto dominance over the complete family.

Lawful outcomes are AUTHORIZED_REPAIR, UNKNOWN_CHOICE_REPAIR, or
UNKNOWN_SEARCH. No-future, evidence-ablation, sham-future, wrong-certificate,
and repair-label-permutation controls are mandatory. Evaluation and selection
calls are charged. No repair is retuned after evaluation.
