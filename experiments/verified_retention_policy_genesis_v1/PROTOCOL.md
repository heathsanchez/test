# Verified Retention-Policy Genesis V1 — Frozen Protocol

## Claim boundary

Test whether a policy for choosing a scarce, dependency-closed executable present can be constructed from a frozen finite grammar, transfer prospectively, receive a certified residual after a regime change, and be revised into a distinct policy that improves an untouched suffix.

This is a finite constructed qualification. It does not test natural-world forecasting, an open-ended policy language, or unrestricted self-modification.

## Precommitment

- Capacity is four nodes.
- Every output must be dependency closed.
- The DSL contains eight set-level features and coefficients in `{-1,0,1}`; all `3^8 = 6561` programs are exhaustively evaluated.
- A program scores whole feasible subsets. The support feature makes the policy non-additive over isolated node rankings.
- Four completed streams authorize initial synthesis; twenty unseen streams test it.
- Four completed changed-regime streams authorize revision; twenty untouched changed-regime streams test the revision.
- Every candidate/authority-stream evaluation is a charged verifier call.
- The old policy is the revision-ablation control.

## Gates

The generated policy must be legal, beat every single-coordinate heuristic and the frozen hand geometry policy prospectively, remain non-oracular, emit a verified policy residual, synthesize a behaviorally distinct revision, and improve the untouched post-change suffix. Removing the revision must restore the old-policy cost.
