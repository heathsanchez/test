# Verified Prospective Value-Evidence Separator Genesis V1

## Frozen claim

Test whether an unresolved policy disagreement can construct evidence that changes policy authority and reduces untouched prospective reconstruction after charging the entire evidence-acquisition path.

## Frozen objects

- The 6,561 policy programs, grammar, budget, objective, original four streams, and verifier are unchanged.
- The unresolved pair is the previously selected policy and the unique geometry-equivalent policy.
- The opaque evidence pool is seeds 1001–1100. The untouched prospective suffix is seeds 401–600.
- The guided constructor scans the pool without reading costs and selects the first eight streams where the two policies choose different retained subsets.
- Random and sham receive the same 200 policy probes, eight external verifier calls, and 78,732 exhaustive resynthesis calls.

## Primary gates

The guided evidence must select the unique geometry-equivalent program. Its prospective cost plus every acquisition call must beat passive, random, and sham. Removing the evidence must restore the passive policy and cost.

This finite constructed qualification does not establish open-ended experiment genesis or inadequacy of the evidence-generation language.
