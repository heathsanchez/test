# Quantum Q7: Constructing missing measurements and quotienting the search engine

**Status:** exact finite laboratory, not a new quantum law or an unrestricted autonomous-development result.

## Objective

Test the seven operational questions against a physical operation model, making the critical distinction between (a) synthesizing an unlisted compound intervention from independently supplied permitted primitive controls, and (b) inventing a genuinely unavailable physical interaction.

### Source / declared world

- One- and two-qubit ideal quantum density matrices; computational-basis projective readout of qubit zero.
- Declared hardware instructions: local `H`, `S`, `Sdg`, and optionally directed `CNOT` gates. Exact arithmetic in SymPy 1.14.0.
- An effect generated by time-ordered circuit `h` is `E(h) = U(h)† (I + Z0) U(h) / 2`.
- Search for a perfect or best available discrimination measurement for independently specified input states `rho`, `sigma` using `|tr(E(h) (rho-sigma))|`.
- A finite effect quotient is synthesized by exhaustive BFS over verified Heisenberg pullbacks `P -> g† P g`, where `P = 2E-I`. A candidate is checked independently by direct dense-matrix circuit replay, and effect signatures are verified using a tomographically complete set of input preparations.

## The exact self-quotient principle

Let `E(h)` be the measurement effect of a circuit and let `g` be an admissible gate inserted *before* `h`. Then

`E(g;h) = g† E(h) g`.

Therefore `E(h1) = E(h2)` implies `E(g;h1) = E(g;h2)`, and, by induction, equality is preserved by **every** future admissible gate-prefix extension. Thus the quotient of circuit-search histories by equal effects is a valid future-sufficient search state, not an arbitrary compression. This uses a mathematical theorem valid for unitaries, not just the finite experiment; its programmatic realization is checked here only for the declared one/two-qubit gate libraries.

## Exact results

- One qubit: six reachable signed Pauli effects; `|+>` vs `|->` separated by `H`, `|+i>` vs `|-i>` by an `S`/`H` program. Removing `H` leaves only `Z` readout and prevents both separations.
- Two qubits: 30 reachable signed nonidentity Pauli effects. Two Bell states with opposite phase are distinguished by a synthesized CNOT/H program. Four held-out nonlocal Pauli-eigenstate pairs (`XY`, `YX`, `ZY`, `YY`) are perfectly separated using synthesized three-gate programs. Without `CNOT` all five pairs remain observationally indistinguishable at the permitted readout even with every non-entangling Clifford circuit.
- Exhaustive 37,449 raw circuits of length at most five collapse to 29 distinct operational effects within the same depth. A further circuit depth reaches the complete, saturated set of 30. All 30 effect representatives are independently replayed with exact dense matrices. Sixteen two-qubit product-state preparations have exactly full tomographic rank and unique signatures for the 30 effects.
- A nonorthogonal control `|0>` vs `|+>` has best achievable Clifford-readout probability difference `1/2`, strictly below the unrestricted Helstrom trace-distance bound `sqrt(2)/2`. The missing optimal basis change is not synthesizable within the declared Clifford readout program language.
- The source verifier kills sign-flipped/corrupted effect certificate claims. An independent raw program enumeration agrees with the depth-restricted quotient.

## Developmental refinement (Q7 obstruction, exact finite witness)

There are **two different quotients** that must not be conflated:

1. **Measurement-program effects:** if two programs implement the same complete operator `E`, adding further unitary gates *before their original program* cannot distinguish them. Equality of complete operators remains safe under the declared extension rule.
2. **Prepared-world states under accessible measurements:** two states can make identical predictions for every *currently* available program, while becoming distinguishable if the system later acquires a new gate. This observational quotient **must be refined** as protected future tests expand.

For the six one-qubit preparations `|0>`, `|1>`, `|+>`, `|->`, `|+i>`, `|-i>`, Z-only-access produces **three** classes, including one class containing all four equatorial states. Enabling Hadamard expands the set of readouts, yielding **six** classes. The previously merged four-state class splits four ways. A deterministic perfect future reconstruction from the old class label alone needs an additional **two bits of retained identifying evidence**, or a new measurement/reacquisition of the world.

For two Bell-phase preparations, local Clifford gates plus the first-qubit readout give **one** class. Adding a permitted CNOT yields **two** classes. The old quotient requires at least **one additional bit**, or an independent observation, to recover the newly observable distinction.

**General mathematical obstruction.** If `F ⊆ F'` are families of protected observations, then `~_{F'}` refines `~_F`. If two states coincide modulo `F` but differ modulo `F'`, there cannot be a well-defined deterministic reconstruction map from their old class to *both* correct new classes: the same input would have to produce two outputs. That is not a failure of compression; it is a proof that reversible provenance or new evidence is necessary for expanding developmental capability.

This exact witness explains why the ``minimal warranted present`` for *today's* protected tests cannot always serve as a sufficient source for *tomorrow's* newly accessible tests. A development architecture must retain reconstructible evidence/certificates or accept future `UNKNOWN`, rather than silently inventing lost distinctions.

## What has **not** been demonstrated

- Reusing the observational quotient as a generator's own search state is a **bounded self-application** of the same reconstruction principle, *not* autonomous invention of the quotient principle or an unsupplied physical gate.
- Equal performance versus a strong, equally source-qualified executable ledger is expected; no unique compounding speedup is claimed.
- Circuit count compression is not a measured execution-time improvement.
- No experimental bath readout, Stuttgart held-out measurements, physical likelihood, noise calibration, or new law of nature is inferred.
- The no-go conclusions cover the specified circuit/readout interface and do not exclude a different device or permitted measurement.
