## 1. Exact no-two-rejections lemma

Let `M=2^107-1`, `A=3^121 mod M`, `U=2^26`. An internal draw is rejected by `zrandom(U)` exactly if the current PRNG state is among the final `U-1` integers immediately below M: `s=M-k` with `1<=k<=U-1`. A second rejection after d raw PRNG steps would require `1 <= A^d k mod M <= U-1`.

For every **d=1 through 22**, the two-dimensional integer lattice generated by `(1, A^d mod M)` and `(0,M)` has **no nonzero vector with both components in [1,U-1]**. Complete sphere enumeration in radius squared `2*(U-1)^2` proves this with exact rational arithmetic, a verified unimodular basis, and no candidate truncation. Since eleven accepted output calls can contain at most eleven accepted draws between any two rejected draws, such a pair would have appeared at a gap <=12. Therefore the complete source eleven-output procedure can contain at most **one** internal rejected draw.

## 2. Source-complete execution schedules

A rejected draw before the first accepted output merely shifts the unobservable initial state and is already covered by V12. The other ten possible schedules are one rejected raw draw immediately before accepted output `j` for `j=1..10`. We tested all ten in the source-native high-limb-first order and reversed-limb control: **20 schedules**.

The P-256 modulus `p=2^256−2^224+2^192+2^96−1` proves 106 precise bit observations invariant for all **2^30** reduction quotients by V12's exact carry/borrow bounds. For each schedule, the observations produce a weighted five-dimensional modular lattice. The solver enumerated all feasible initial states using exact integer-root bounds and direct PRNG replay. Across 20 schedules it obtained **24 partial-state solutions**, all checked against the eleven full accepted outputs, but **zero** reproduce the P-256 public generator x-coordinate, even before the separate rejected/accepted-state guards.

Six positive controls placed a genuine rejected FreeLIP state `M-1` at positions 1, 7, and 10, in both limb orders. The solver reconstructed the correct hidden state, matched the complete output, and detected the exact rejected draw.
