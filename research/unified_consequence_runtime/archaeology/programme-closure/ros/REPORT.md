* Experiment-language source explicitly sets `target=(0,1,1,0)` and its main verifies a separate `qstar=lambda x,y:x^y`, although its snapshot says `target_not_supplied=true`. Preserve that conflicting record. It is not target-free autonomous new-language discovery.

* Prospective-repair `choose` is generic. The original `consequence` fixture compares against a hash-derived target partition. Importing `choose` need not import that target or fabricate new native success from its old fixture.
* Retention geometry is hand-authored. Policy construction did not outperform it: 104,887 versus 102,558 held-out cost. The representability result rules out language inadequacy as the explanation for that exact gap.
* Value-evidence acquisition uses supplied geometry and synthetic stream definitions. Its useful lesson is to seek distinguishing evidence and reselect within the existing grammar before extending it.
* Semantic-certificate cost evidence concerns supplied finite role encodings, not automatic correspondence discovery or unrestricted cross-representation transport.
